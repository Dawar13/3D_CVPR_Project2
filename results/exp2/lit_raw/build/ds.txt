  gt_depth: via LiDAR projection (devkit has RS-aware projection)

  imu: NO raw IMU (velocity per image)
  gs_twin: NO
  indoor_outdoor: outdoor driving (urban/suburban)
  dynamic: YES (traffic, pedestrians)
  size: 1150 scenes x 20 s (CVPR'20 paper); later versions and GB UNVERIFIED (behind login)
  license: Waymo Dataset License Agreement for Non-Commercial Use (March 2025); devkit Apache-2.0; camera model code under src/waymo_open_dataset/wdl_limited/ with its own LICENSE
  download: Google sign-in + accept license (waymo.com/open/data/perception redirects to Google OAuth)
  needs_human: YES
  timestamp_convention: proto comment: desired timestamp per column = middle of exposure of that column; 'time image center (= middle of exposure of middle row)'. Devkit: t(row) = camera_trigger_time + 0.5*shutter + readout*row/H (TOP_TO_BOTTOM). pose given at pose_timestamp
  verified: YES (proto + devkit source + paper); per-image readout values UNVERIFIED
  notes: Best-documented RS timing of any real dataset. Fields: CameraImage.pose(3), velocity(4), pose_timestamp(5), shutter(6), camera_trigger_time(7), camera_readout_done_time(8); CameraCalibration.rolling_shutter_direction(6).
